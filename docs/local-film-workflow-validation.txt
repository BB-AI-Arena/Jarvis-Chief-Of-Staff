Jarvis local narrative film workflow — November 11–12, 2026

Implemented
- Full user brief and exact requested duration, bounded to 600 seconds. Native planning/review token caps scale with duration from 150,000 to 4 million. This development test includes earlier rejected prompt/scene experiments and hit that cap after 60 accepted clips; one finite extension to 6 million was applied only after the old worker stopped, preserving the checkpoint. Grok still requests remain capped at ten and no cloud video endpoint is used.
- Artwork is selected from the mapped project source; spaced, compound and hyphenated project names resolve consistently.
- The approved cinematic face/hood has a clean reference crop. Original artwork files are unchanged.
- Local Projects outline; Creative film bible and distinct timed screenplay events; detailed shots are directed against causal in/out states instead of restarting the same mini-story every 30 seconds.
- Supporting cast reference portraits are generated locally and reused in later shots.
- FLUX.2 Klein 4B distilled, four steps, CFG 1 builds physical opening scenes on DARK. Only visible people/props enter a scene prompt; the entire offscreen cast is not copied into every generation prompt.
- Repeated local failures trigger a practical director rewrite and optional local Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 (20B) repair before bounded Grok still repair. Local masked repairs preserve correct pixels elsewhere. A ten-minute job reserves at most ten Grok still requests. A lost response still consumes its reservation.
- LTX-2.3 animates on DARK. Motion repairs use restrained camera targets and local start/end-frame guidance. Guide latents enter before AV concat and are cropped before video decode.
- Repaired motion drives both ending-keyframe generation and temporal review. Opening pose/view is not treated as permanent appearance. Ending frames must show the required physical change. A bounded alternative staging can reuse a sound ending pose only after independent story/action review; the actual repaired direction is checkpointed and recorded in the final report.
- Every new motion take is directed from its reviewed actual opening before rendering. Source-valid two-handed grips are allowed even when the draft wanted one hand; loaded phone profiles stay stable instead of being rebuilt. Explicit user requirements, identity, sound anatomy and meaningful action remain binding.
- Ending direction may simplify an assistant-authored tap/trace/withdraw cycle into one changed pose, with the revised motion used for generation, review, caching and reporting. Microscopic smudges and displaced droplets are not used as substitutes for visible performance. Equivalent study gestures may satisfy an assistant-authored detail beat; explicit user actions still bind.
- PCM edit intermediates preserve exact cut boundaries without accumulating per-shot AAC padding. Completion checks duration, 720p, audio, 24 fps and the exact expected frame count, and is saved only after the report and final storyboard exist.
- The final AAC master explicitly uses 48 kHz stereo and checks decoded true peak below -1 dBTP. A bounded gain correction, if needed for codec overshoot, is re-encoded from the uncompressed mix. Final completion also checks those audio properties.
- A complete-film narration pass writes consistent narration from the causal timeline, without recycling the draft slogans. Candidate scripts and bounded word/timing repairs are cached. New narration remains intact when the visual director changes blocking.
- After bounded image attempts, the director may reblock a usable frame around its narrative function, including an aspect-preserving camera crop of at least 30 percent of the source to exclude nonessential peripheral artifacts, preserving user requirements, source identities and sound anatomy; it is independently reviewed before acceptance. Full plans and intermediate visual overrides are saved so resuming does not lose a successful rewrite.
- Local Kokoro narration is timed before the full render. An original evolving underscore and cues are mixed with scene ambience. DARK NVENC creates the final 720p/24 fps picture.
- When the native ACE-Step 1.5 checkpoint is present, Creative directs an original instrumental score with a shared palette, motif, tempo and key. Long jobs use overlapping local cues. Stereo crossfades and narration ducking are mixed locally; partial score jobs and the finished score are checkpointed. The synthesized underscore remains the explicit fallback when the music model is absent.
- A failed phone display can receive bounded local post-production: a close-crop plane estimate is refined against case edges, then LK/RANSAC tracks the reference plane through the generated actor performance. Bare foreground fingers retain occlusion. Unreliable tracks fail closed; the composed result must independently pass the same visual/action review. A Hit List confirmation is an optional header graphic, never a crown on the toy. This is not used to replace character movement with a moving still.
- Screen repair is available after either video take, including an ending-guided repair. Internal ending guides may contain temporary display-lettering defects when pose, identity and phone geometry are sound; final display/identity/action review remains mandatory. Timed narration supplies narrative context without claiming an audio audit. Source/plane/script hashes give repaired outputs distinct filenames so later candidates cannot overwrite an accepted take.
- Temporal review distinguishes essential user events from incidental assistant camera/facing directions, while rejecting real identity changes, missing performance and incoherent scene jumps. Ending guides must preserve set geometry and cannot silently mirror the street/shop layout.
- Before re-rendering, an editor may propose equivalent blocking from an actual sound take, subject to an independent story/action review. It cannot trade essential events for generic reactions, excuse bad anatomy/identity, or accept a static actor. If bounded motion/end-guide repairs still fail, one reserved starting-scene restage is available per shot; network/budget errors do not trigger it. Accepted work remains checkpointed.
- Occasional character-free establishing/POV coverage can omit artwork references only with environment_only:true. Character/product shots still require actual identity references. Environment coverage needs genuine movement (for example traffic/doors) and cannot substitute for an essential user-requested action. Difficult mascot locomotion can instead use boot-level coverage, a rear silhouette or an appropriate establishing cut when adjacent coverage supplies the arrival.
- Failed visual critiques receive one bounded adjudication against the actual story purpose before repair. Valid profile views, natural grips and readable medium-shot phone gestures are allowed; true anatomy, identity and essential variant errors remain failures.
- Qwen keeps all selected references as separate images. For three references it regenerates the scene without a failed-scene anchor, preserving the last prop reference and avoiding collage-board leakage. Masked Flux repair can retain the anchor plus all three source references.
- Cast references exclude incidental handheld props; a face/upper-collar crop prevents an unrelated toy in a canonical portrait from contaminating later phone screens and figures.
- Checkpoints retain accepted scenes, clips, cast, direction repairs and accepted Comfy prompt IDs; mismatched stories are rejected. Cancellation targets this job's queued/running prompt only.
- Native progress is streamed while the worker runs, not buffered until exit. Worker imports do not modify the signed app bundle.
- Confirmed cancellation clears the canceled pending prompt, so a resumed job does not attach to a prompt it already interrupted.
- Claude supplies story direction and sampled visual review. Grok is a bounded still repair service. This is not an entirely offline pipeline. No cloud video endpoint is called by the worker.

Quality limits
A successful generation is not a quality pass. Opening images and temporal samples are reviewed, with bounded corrections. Sampled review cannot prove every frame or every audio event is perfect. This test is not evidence of guaranteed Hollywood-level quality.
