# THE EXACT ONE
This is the approval screenplay for a Drop Assassin concept film: Creative Director revision 3, 2026-11-13. It is unapproved and Research has not countersigned it. No assets, renders or file changes.

The planned runtime is 6:58: an opening card and eight scenes at 24 fps, with no narrator. That is a timing plan, not a measured cut.

## DIRECTION
**Night Case.** One apartment, two evenings and one face. There are two exact items, and each has one job:
- **The drop:** Thrilljoy Violet Lantern Standard. Alex misses it with a prior app, then catches its restock with Drop Assassin.
- **The chase:** Thrilljoy Violet Lantern Chase. Alex enters it in Hunt and finds it in another member's collection.

**Palette (proposed hex values):** black, charcoal cards, gold highlights and a restrained red.
- Night #0A0A0C
- Card #1A1C22
- Gold #D6B25E
- Alert red #C43232
- Prior-app and store paper #F7F7F5, ink #161616

These follow the brand direction cited in earlier drafts. I could not open the brand file this session, so Research must check them against it.

**Type and logo.** UI text uses a real condensed italic grotesque typeface. It is typeset and composited, never generated. The logo is used only if Brandon supplies the approved file, and it is never redrawn.

**Motion.** Hard cuts, one 12-frame dissolve into the second evening, and push-ins on the phone UI. There are no alert-edge pulses or flashes.

**Alternates.**
- **A:** Retitle the film *Second Chance*, keeping the same script.
- **B:** A warm daylight pass with the same cuts and words. It is easier to shoot, and easier to forget.

## STATUS LABELS (one rule for the whole film)
1. **Opening card.** It says that this is a concept film, that every app screen is a designed plate rather than a capture, and that marked screens show planned features.
2. **Gold "Upcoming preview" chip.** It marks planned features from Brandon's vision:
   - the alert-speed goal
   - scan-to-confirm
   - collection value
   - imports
   - cross-source Hunt results
3. **"Concept sequence. Not a shipped screen." caption.** It marks the whole member path: the listing, offer, interest notice, acceptance and contact sharing.
4. **Unmarked plates.** Intel, Body Count, Hunt and Lock On are shown without a chip. Before any render, Research must confirm that each of these screens has shipped. Any screen that is not confirmed gets the chip.
5. **Store and stock plates.** Every store page and stock situation carries "Story plate. Not a recorded stock event." No store is named.
6. **Placeholder product names.** Every collectible name is a production placeholder: Violet Lantern Standard, Violet Lantern Chase, Harbor Lantern Standard and Redline Racer. Brandon either approves them as fictional names or swaps in verified references before any render. The film claims no edition size, MSRP, market value or coverage percentage.

- One silver stud, in the left ear only.
- The same navy hoodie (#1B2430) on both nights.
- Five fingers on each hand, and natural ears, jaw and elbows.
- One black phone in the same uncracked case, held in his right hand.

**Jordan.** Jordan Hale appears only as the initials JH on a charcoal circle (#243044) and is never on camera. Jordan's phone appears once, as a screen-only insert with no hand or person in frame.

**Readable text.**
- Every readable word is a typeset or captured plate, composited in focus, and comes only from the copy deck.
- Latin characters only. Generated frames contain nothing legible.
- Background boxes are turned away, with their spines soft.
- Funko Pop!, Thrilljoy and eBay are set as plain text in the film typeface, never as redrawn logos.

**What never appears.**
- Glow, colour or pack price never identifies a chase.
- No currency symbol, price, stock count, stopwatch, timestamp or latency readout.
- No signed or autographed items.
- Drop Assassin never takes payment, holds an item or ships one.
